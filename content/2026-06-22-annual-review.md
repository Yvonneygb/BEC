# BEC Daily | Annual Review

## Dialogue

**A (Rebecca Torres — Department Head, MarketEdge Consulting)**
**B (David Zhang — Marketing Specialist, MarketEdge Consulting)**

**A:** David, welcome to your annual performance review. I want to start by saying this has been a remarkable year for you — your campaign analytics work alone drove a 23% uptick in client retention. How do you feel the year has gone from your perspective?

**B:** Thank you, Rebecca. I'm proud of what we've accomplished on the analytics front. That said, I do feel there were areas where I could have stretched myself more — particularly in new business development. I tended to stay in my comfort zone with existing accounts rather than pursuing leads.

**A:** That's impressively self-aware, and actually ties into one of my key points today. Your core competency in data-driven marketing is outstanding, but for the next level, we need to see more initiative on the business development side. Let's set some concrete objectives around that.

**B:** I'd welcome that structure. One thing I'd like to raise is professional development. I've identified a certification programme in strategic marketing that I believe would directly benefit my KPIs next year. Would the department consider sponsoring it?

**A:** I've actually got your training request on my desk. The budget is tight this quarter, but I can approve 70% sponsorship if you commit to sharing the learnings with the wider team through a workshop series. That way we get a multiplier effect on the investment.

**B:** That's more than fair — I'll take that. On the topic of team contribution, I've also been mentoring our two junior associates on A/B testing methodology. I'd like that to be formally recognised as part of my appraisal criteria going forward.

**A:** Noted, and I appreciate the initiative. Now, let's talk numbers. Based on your performance metrics, I'm recommending a 12% salary increase and a promotion to Senior Marketing Specialist, effective next month. The promotion comes with an expanded scope — you'll own the Q3 product launch end-to-end.

**B:** That's incredibly motivating to hear, Rebecca. I'm genuinely excited about leading the Q3 launch. Could we also discuss the possibility of a flexible working arrangement? Even two days remote per week would significantly boost my productivity based on the data from our trial period last autumn.

**A:** I'm open to it. Let's pilot a hybrid schedule — three days in office, two remote — with a review after 90 days. If the Q3 launch metrics hold up, we'll make it permanent. I'll need you to document your workflow so others can learn from it.

**B:** Absolutely — I'll put together a remote collaboration playbook based on what worked during the trial. And Rebecca, I genuinely appreciate how thorough this review has been. It's the first time I've walked out of an appraisal feeling I have a clear roadmap rather than just a rating.

**A:** That's exactly the experience we're aiming for, David. You've earned this. Let's reconvene in six months for a mid-year pulse check. Congratulations — you've set a high bar for the rest of the team.

## Key Vocabulary

- **performance review** — 绩效评估/年终考核 | 年度正式评估，也称 annual appraisal，BEC核心商务场景
- **core competency** — 核心竞争力 | 个人或企业最擅长的领域，阅读和写作高频词
- **KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)** — 关键绩效指标 | 评估体系中量化目标的核心概念
- **professional development** — 职业发展/专业进修 | 包括培训、认证等系统化成长路径
- **appraisal criteria** — 评估标准 | 绩效评估中用于衡量员工表现的具体维度
- **sponsorship** — 资助/赞助（培训费用） | 公司为员工培训提供财务支持
- **performance metrics** — 绩效指标/量化数据 | 评估中可量化的成绩衡量维度
- **promotion** — 晋升/升职 | 年度评估的主要成果之一，BEC口语高频词
- **hybrid schedule** — 混合办公制 | 部分时间在办公室、部分时间远程的工作模式
- **playbook** — 操作手册/指南 | 将成功经验系统化记录以供团队参考

## Sentence Patterns

- **How do you feel the year has gone from your perspective?** → 从你的角度看，这一年过得怎么样？ | 绩效面谈开场，鼓励员工先自我评估
- **That ties into one of my key points today.** → 这正好关联到我今天要说的一个重点 | 将员工的自我认知与管理层观察自然衔接
- **I'd welcome that structure.** → 我欢迎这样的框架/安排 | 礼貌表达对上级提议的积极接受
- **Would the department consider sponsoring it?** → 部门会考虑资助吗？ | 委婉提出培训经费请求，比直接要求更商务化
- **That's more than fair — I'll take that.** → 这很公道——我接受 | 谈判或协商中积极回应对方让步的表达
- **You've set a high bar for the rest of the team.** → 你为团队其他人树立了高标准 | 年终评估中的正面收尾，激励性表达

## Chinese Translation

**A:** David，欢迎来到你的年度绩效评估。我想先说，这一年对你来说是卓越的一年——仅你的营销活动分析工作就推动了客户留任率提升23%。从你的角度看，这一年过得怎么样？

**B:** 谢谢你，Rebecca。我为我们在分析方面取得的成就感到自豪。话虽如此，我确实觉得有些领域我本可以更进一步——尤其是在新业务拓展方面。我倾向于待在现有客户的舒适区，而不是主动开发潜在客户。

**A:** 这个自我认知令人印象深刻，而且正好关联到我今天要说的一个重点。你在数据驱动营销方面的核心竞争力非常出色，但要迈向下一个层级，我们需要看到你在业务拓展方面展现出更多主动性。让我们围绕这一点制定一些具体目标。

**B:** 我很欢迎这样的框架安排。有件事我想提一下——职业发展。我发现了一个战略营销认证课程，我相信它会直接影响我明年的KPI。部门会考虑资助吗？

**A:** 你的培训申请已经在我桌上了。本季度预算紧张，但我可以批准70%的资助，前提是你要通过系列工作坊与整个团队分享所学内容。这样我们就能获得投资的乘数效应。

**B:** 这很公道——我接受。关于团队贡献，我也一直在辅导两位初级同事学习A/B测试方法论。我希望这能在今后的评估标准中被正式认可。

**A:** 已记录，我很欣赏你的主动性。现在，我们来谈数字。基于你的绩效指标，我推荐12%的薪资涨幅，并晋升为高级营销专员，下月生效。晋升附带扩大的职责范围——你将全程负责第三季度产品发布。

**B:** 这太令人振奋了，Rebecca。我对主导第三季度发布充满期待。我们能否也讨论一下灵活工作安排的可能性？哪怕每周两天远程，根据我们去年的试行数据，也会显著提高我的效率。

**A:** 我对此持开放态度。我们来试行混合办公制——三天办公室、两天远程——90天后评估。如果第三季度发布指标达标，我们就转为永久安排。我需要你记录工作流程，方便其他人学习。

**B:** 没问题——我会基于试行期间的成功经验，整理一份远程协作操作手册。Rebecca，我由衷感谢这次评估如此详尽。这是我第一次在考核结束后感觉自己拿到的是清晰的路线图，而不仅仅是一个分数。

**A:** 这正是我们追求的面谈体验，David。这是你应得的。六个月后我们再做一次中期回顾。恭喜——你为团队其他人树立了高标准。

## Learning Tip

年度评估（Annual Review）是BEC口语Part 2常考的"一对一商务沟通"场景。建议重点练习两大话术：一是"接受反馈"——用 That's a fair point / I'll take that on board 替代简单说 OK；二是"主动提出发展需求"——用 I'd like to raise... / Would you consider... 等委婉结构，而非直接的 I want...。同时，熟悉 KPI、appraisal、competency 等评估体系术语对听力和阅读至关重要。
