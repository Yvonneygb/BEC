BEC Daily

质量保证 Quality Assurance

A (Emily Zhang) — QA Manager, MedTech Devices
B (Robert Hayes) — Production Director, MedTech Devices

Dialogue

A:Robert, I've just received the preliminary findings from our ISO 13485 surveillance audit, and I need to brief you before the management review meeting. There are three non-conformities — one of them is classified as major.
Robert，我刚收到ISO 13485监督审核的初步报告，需要在管理评审会之前向你汇报。有三项不合格——其中一项被定级为严重不合格。
B:A major non-conformity? That's the last thing we need ahead of the FDA inspection next month. Walk me through it — which process area triggered it?
严重不合格？在下个月FDA检查之前这绝对是我们最不想看到的事情。给我详细说一遍——是哪个流程区域触发它的？
A:The sterile packaging validation for Batch 2407. The auditor found that our process validation records were incomplete — specifically, the bioburden testing data for three production runs was documented but not formally signed off by the quality director. It's a paperwork issue, but under ISO 13485, incomplete validation documentation is an automatic major finding.
批次2407的无菌包装验证。审核员发现我们的过程验证记录不完整——具体来说，三个生产批次的微生物负荷测试数据有记录，但缺少质量总监的正式签批。这属于文件问题，但按ISO 13485标准，验证记录不完整自动构成严重不合格项。
B:That's frustrating. The testing was done — we have the raw data. It's the sign-off workflow that failed. What's the corrective action timeline?
这令人沮丧。测试是完成了的——我们有原始数据。出问题的是签批流程。纠正措施的时间线是怎样的？
A:We have 30 days to submit a corrective action plan to the certification body. I'm proposing a three-pronged approach: first, immediate retrospective sign-off for the affected batches with full traceability documentation; second, a root cause analysis to understand why the sign-off was missed; third, implementing an electronic quality management system to enforce digital sign-off gates going forward.
我们有30天时间向认证机构提交纠正措施计划。我建议采取三管齐下的方案：第一，对受影响批次进行即时回溯签批，并附完整的可追溯性文件；第二，进行根本原因分析，查明签批遗漏的原因；第三，实施电子质量管理系统，在后续流程中强制执行数字化签批关卡。
B:The eQMS has been on our wish list for two years. If the major non-conformity gives us the leverage to finally get budget approval, there's a silver lining. What about the other two findings?
eQMS在我们的愿望清单上已经躺了两年了。如果这项严重不合格能成为我们最终获得预算批准的筹码，那倒是一线希望。另外两项不合格呢？
A:Both are minor. One relates to calibration records for two measurement instruments that were overdue by three weeks. The other is a training gap — two operators on the assembly line hadn't completed their annual GMP refresher course. Both are straightforward to close out.
两项都是轻微不合格。一项涉及两台测量仪器的校准记录逾期三周。另一项是培训缺口——装配线上两名操作员没有完成年度GMP进修课程。两项都很容易结案。
B:Right. Let's tackle the calibration issue immediately — have maintenance recalibrate the instruments and update the log by end of day. For the training gap, schedule the GMP refresher for early next week and update the training matrix. On the major finding, I want a draft root cause analysis on my desk by Friday.
好。校准问题立即处理——让维护部门今天内重新校准仪器并更新日志。培训缺口方面，安排下周初的GMP进修课并更新培训矩阵。关于严重不合格，我要在周五前拿到根本原因分析的初稿。
A:Agreed. I'll coordinate with the document control team on the retrospective sign-offs and start building the business case for the eQMS. One more thing — the auditor hinted that our continuous improvement metrics could be stronger. He suggested we implement statistical process control on our three most critical production lines.
同意。我会与文件控制团队协调回溯签批，同时开始构建eQMS的商业论证。还有一件事——审核员暗示我们的持续改进指标可以做得更好。他建议我们在三条最关键的产线上实施统计过程控制。
B:SPC is a best practice we should have adopted years ago. Let's include it in the quality improvement plan. If we present the entire package — corrective actions plus preventive measures — the management team is more likely to approve the eQMS investment.
SPC是我们早该采用的最佳实践。把它纳入质量改进计划中。如果我们把整套方案——纠正措施加预防措施——一起提报，管理团队更有可能批准eQMS的投资。
A:That's a smart framing. I'll prepare a consolidated presentation covering all findings, corrective actions, and the preventive roadmap. Can we schedule a dry run on Monday before the formal management review on Wednesday?
这个包装方式很聪明。我会准备一份整合的演示文稿，涵盖所有不合格项、纠正措施和预防路线图。周三正式管理评审前，周一我们能不能先进行一轮预演？
B:Let's do it. Monday at 2 PM in the main conference room. And Emily — don't beat yourself up over the major finding. These audits are designed to identify gaps. What matters is how we respond.
就这么办。周一下午2点，主会议室。还有Emily——别为严重不合格太自责。这些审核的本意就是发现差距。关键是我们如何应对。
A:Thanks, Robert. I'll have the materials ready.
谢谢，Robert。我会把材料准备好。

Key Vocabulary

non-conformity — 不合格项 | 质量管理体系中不符合标准要求的发现项
surveillance audit — 监督审核 | 认证机构对获证组织进行的定期合规检查
root cause analysis — 根本原因分析 | 追溯问题深层原因的系统性调查方法
corrective action — 纠正措施 | 消除不合格项根本原因以防止再发生的行动计划
electronic quality management system (eQMS) — 电子质量管理系统 | 数字化管理质量流程和文档的软件平台
traceability — 可追溯性 | 追踪产品从原材料到成品全过程记录的能力
statistical process control (SPC) — 统计过程控制 | 用统计方法监控和控制生产过程的质控工具
GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) — 良好生产规范 | 确保产品持续按质量标准生产的体系
close out — 关闭/结案 | 在质量管理中表示不合格项已完成整改并验证通过
continuous improvement — 持续改进 | 质量管理中不断优化流程和产品的核心理念

Sentence Patterns

Walk me through it — ... → 给我详细说一遍——…… | 要求对方按步骤解释复杂事项的高效句式
That's the last thing we need ahead of... → 在……之前这绝对是我们最不想要的事 | 强调在某个关键时刻之前某个问题的严重性
If X gives us the leverage to Y, there's a silver lining. → 如果X给了我们做Y的筹码，那倒是一线希望 | 在负面情境中找出积极面的经典表达
Both are straightforward to close out. → 这两项都很容易结案 | 质量管理中评估问题难度的简洁判定
That's a smart framing. → 这个包装方式很聪明 | 认可对方呈现方案策略的常用赞语
Don't beat yourself up over... → 别为……太自责 | 商务沟通中安慰和鼓励同事的贴心表达

Learning Tip

质量保证（Quality Assurance）是BEC听力和阅读考试中的高难度主题，涉及大量专业术语和流程描述。建议建立自己的"QA词汇地图"：将术语按类别分组——审核（audit, non-conformity, corrective action）、文档（traceability, validation, sign-off）、工具（SPC, eQMS, root cause analysis）。BEC阅读Part 3经常出现QA场景的流程排序题，熟悉这些术语能让你快速定位关键信息。
