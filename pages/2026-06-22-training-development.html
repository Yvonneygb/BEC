培训发展 Training & Development

📅 2026年6月22日 · 星期一
A (Linda Park) — HR Director, Nexus Solutions
B (James Carter) — Learning & Development Manager, Nexus Solutions

Dialogue

A:James, I've just come from the quarterly board meeting. The CEO is pushing for a more aggressive digital upskilling programme across all departments. He wants to see a comprehensive proposal on my desk by Friday. Where do we stand on our current training needs assessment?
James，我刚开完季度董事会回来。CEO正在力推一项覆盖所有部门的、更激进的数据化技能提升计划，要求周五前看到一份完整的方案。我们目前的培训需求评估进展如何？
B:I've actually been anticipating this, Linda. Our employee engagement survey flagged a significant skills gap in data analytics and AI literacy — nearly 62% of our mid-level managers rated themselves as "below proficient." I've drafted a preliminary competency framework, but I'll need your sign-off before we commission the external training provider.
其实我一直在提前准备，Linda。我们的员工敬业度调查反映出一个显著的技能缺口，集中在数据分析和人工智能素养方面——将近62%的中层管理者自评"不够熟练"。我已经草拟了一份初步的能力框架，但在正式选定外部培训供应商之前需要你的批准。
A:Sixty-two percent is higher than I expected. That's a red flag, especially with the ERP migration coming in Q3. What's the projected cost for a company-wide rollout?
62%比我预想的要高。这是个危险信号，特别是考虑到第三季度即将进行ERP系统迁移。全公司推广的预计成本是多少？
B:I've shortlisted three vendors. The most promising one — TechBridge Academy — has quoted £48,000 for a blended learning programme covering 120 employees across six cohorts. That includes on-the-job coaching, e-learning modules, and a capstone project. Per head, that works out to about £400, which is well within our benchmark.
我筛选了三家供应商。最有前景的一家——TechBridge学院——报价48000英镑，提供为期六期的混合式学习计划，覆盖120名员工。包括在岗辅导、在线学习模块和一个毕业项目。人均成本约400英镑，远在我们的基准线以内。
A:£48,000 is reasonable, but I'm more concerned about ROI. The last sales training programme we ran had a completion rate of just 54%. How do we ensure this one actually sticks?
48000英镑尚可接受，但我更关心投资回报率。我们上次的销售培训项目完成率只有54%。这次怎么确保真正落地？
B:That's a fair concern, and I've factored it in. TechBridge uses a flipped classroom model with weekly check-ins and a dedicated learning management system to track progress. They also provide post-training performance metrics — things like productivity gains and error rate reduction — measured at the 30, 60 and 90-day marks. Our pilot group last quarter showed a 23% improvement in task efficiency.
这个顾虑很合理，我已经考虑进去了。TechBridge采用翻转课堂模式，配有每周检查和专用的学习管理系统来追踪进度。他们还提供培训后的绩效数据——比如生产力提升和错误率下降——分别在30天、60天和90天节点进行衡量。上季度的试点小组在任务效率上提升了23%。
A:That data helps build the business case. One more thing — the board specifically asked about leadership development for our succession planning pipeline. We have twelve high-potential employees earmarked for senior roles within 18 months.
这些数据有助于支撑商业论证。还有一件事——董事会特别问到了针对继任计划渠道的领导力发展。我们有十二名高潜力员工被纳入18个月内晋升高级岗位的名单。
B:I was going to raise that. I'd propose a separate leadership track — a six-month programme with executive coaching, cross-functional project assignments, and 360-degree feedback loops. The investment per participant would be around £2,800, but given the cost of external senior hires, the payoff is substantial.
我正要提这个。我建议单独设立领导力培养轨道——为期六个月的项目，包含高管辅导、跨职能项目轮岗和360度反馈。每位参与者的投入大约2800英镑，但考虑到外部招聘高管的成本，回报是相当可观的。
A:Let's bundle both proposals. Present the digital upskilling as Phase One and the leadership track as Phase Two. I can push for Q3 budget allocation if the timeline is clearly mapped out. Can you have the finalised deck ready by Thursday afternoon?
我们把两个方案打包提报。将数字技能提升定位为第一阶段，领导力发展作为第二阶段。如果时间线清晰，我可以争取第三季度的预算。周四下午前你能把最终版演示文稿准备好吗？
B:Absolutely. I'll polish the slide deck and include the pilot group data, the vendor comparison matrix, and a five-year cost-benefit projection. I'll also circulate a one-page executive summary beforehand so the board can digest the key figures at a glance.
没问题。我会完善幻灯片，加入试点小组数据、供应商对比矩阵和五年成本效益预测。同时事先传阅一份一页的执行摘要，方便董事会快速掌握关键数据。
A:Perfect. And James — good work staying ahead of this. It makes my job a lot easier when L&D is proactive rather than reactive.
很好。James，你能提前做这些准备工作做得不错。这样我的工作就容易多了——学习发展部主动出击总比被动应对好。
B:Thanks, Linda. I'll drop the materials in your inbox by 3 PM Thursday.
谢谢，Linda。材料周四下午3点前发到你邮箱。

Key Vocabulary

upskilling — 技能提升 | 帮助员工学习新技能以适应岗位变化的培训
competency framework — 能力框架 | 定义岗位所需技能和知识的结构化模型
blended learning — 混合式学习 | 结合线上和线下教学的培训模式
learning management system (LMS) — 学习管理系统 | 用于管理、追踪和交付培训课程的软件平台
flipped classroom — 翻转课堂 | 学员课前自学理论，课堂上进行实践和讨论的教学模式
succession planning — 继任计划 | 为企业关键岗位培养后备人才的长远规划
360-degree feedback — 360度反馈 | 从上级、同事、下属等多角度收集的绩效评估
cost-benefit projection — 成本效益预测 | 对培训投入与预期收益的量化分析
business case — 商业论证 | 论证项目投资合理性的正式文件

Sentence Patterns

Where do we stand on...? → 我们在……方面进展如何？ | 会议中询问项目或任务当前状态的常用句式
I've shortlisted three vendors. → 我筛选出了三家供应商 | 表达经过初步筛选后形成的候选名单
That's a red flag. → 这是一个危险信号 | 用于指出值得警惕的问题或风险
How do we ensure this one actually sticks? → 我们如何确保这次能真正落地？ | 质疑培训效果持续性的实用表达
The payoff is substantial. → 回报是相当可观的 | 商务场景中强调投资回报的经典说法
I can push for Q3 budget allocation if... → 如果……我就可以争取第三季度的预算 | 讨论预算争取条件的实用句型

Learning Tip

在BEC商务英语考试中，培训与发展（Training & Development）是口语Part 2和写作Part 2的常见主题。建议多积累与ROI（投资回报率）相关的表达，如"measure the effectiveness""track learning outcomes""cost per participant"等，因为考官常要求你评估培训方案的价值。同时注意区分不同类型的培训术语：on-the-job training（在岗培训）、off-site training（脱产培训）、e-learning（在线学习）等，这些在阅读和听力中高频出现。
