高管薪酬 Executive Compensation

A (Catherine Blake) — Compensation Committee Chair, Atlas Industries Board
B (Mark Thompson) — External Compensation Consultant, Mercer Advisors

Dialogue

A:Mark, the board has asked me to lead this year's executive compensation review, and I want your independent perspective before we finalise the figures. Our CEO, David Hartford, has delivered a solid 18% total shareholder return this year. The question is: how does that performance translate into a defensible compensation package?
Mark，董事会让我负责今年的高管薪酬审查，在最终敲定数字之前，我想听取你的独立意见。我们的CEO David Hartford今年实现了18%的稳健股东总回报。问题是：这样的业绩如何转化为一个经得起推敲的薪酬方案？
B:Happy to provide that, Catherine. I've completed the benchmarking analysis against 22 peer companies in the industrial manufacturing sector. At David's current total compensation of £2.4 million — base salary £650,000, annual bonus £520,000, and long-term incentives valued at £1.23 million — he sits at the 55th percentile. Given the TSR performance you mentioned, there's a case for moving him toward the 65th.
很高兴提供这个意见，Catherine。我已经完成了对工业制造板块22家对标公司的薪酬对比分析。David目前的薪酬总额为240万英镑——基本工资65万、年度奖金52万、长期激励价值123万——处于第55百分位。考虑到你提到的股东总回报表现，有理由将他的薪酬提升到65百分位左右。
A:The board is generally comfortable with the 65th percentile if we can tie it tightly to performance metrics that align with long-term shareholder value. What I'd like to avoid is a package that rewards short-term stock price bumps over sustainable growth. Can we structure the long-term incentive plan accordingly?
如果能将薪酬与符合长期股东价值的业绩指标紧密挂钩，董事会大体上可以接受第65百分位。我希望避免的是奖励短期股价飙升而非可持续增长的方案。能否据此设计长期激励计划？
B:Absolutely. I recommend a three-pronged LTI approach. First, performance shares with a three-year vesting period, tied to relative TSR and return on invested capital. Second, a deferred bonus scheme where 40% of the annual bonus is held back for two years and subject to clawback if performance reverses. Third, a modest increase in the base salary — perhaps £690,000 — to reflect inflation adjustments, but no more.
完全可以。我建议采用三管齐下的长期激励方案。第一，三年归属期的绩效股，与相对股东总回报和投入资本回报率挂钩。第二，递延奖金计划——40%年度奖金延迟两年发放，如果业绩反转则受追回条款约束。第三，基本工资适度上调——或许到69万英镑——以反映通胀调整，但不宜更多。
A:I particularly like the deferred bonus with clawback provision — that directly addresses shareholder concerns about executives walking away with large payouts after short-term wins. What about the optics? Even a well-structured £2.8 million package attracts scrutiny. How are peer companies managing the narrative?
我特别赞同附带追回条款的递延奖金——这直接回应了股东对高管短期获利后带着大笔报酬离职的担忧。公众形象怎么处理？即便是精心设计的280万英镑方案也会招致审视。同行企业是如何应对叙事的？
B:Transparency is the best defence. The companies that avoid backlash are those that publish a clear pay-for-performance rationale in their annual report — showing the link between each compensation element and a specific strategic goal. I'd also recommend we benchmark against the median employee pay ratio. Atlas currently stands at 72:1, which is well within the acceptable range for the sector.
透明度是最好的防御。成功避免反弹的公司都会在年度报告中公开清晰的"绩效薪酬"逻辑——展示每一薪酬要素与具体战略目标之间的联系。我还建议对标员工薪酬中位数比率。Atlas目前是72:1，远在行业可接受范围内。
A:Good — let's include the pay ratio disclosure. One more thing: David's contract is up for renewal, and there's been board discussion about a retention package. Given that three of our competitors have tried to poach him this year, we may need a golden handcuff of some sort.
好——把薪酬比率披露纳入。还有一件事：David的合同即将到期续签，董事会讨论过留任方案。考虑到今年有三家竞争对手试图挖他，我们可能需要某种"金手铐"。
B:Retention is a legitimate concern. Rather than a golden parachute — which can send the wrong signal — I'd suggest a retention equity grant of £500,000 in restricted stock units, vesting over four years in equal tranches. It's tied to continued service, not performance, which avoids the perverse incentive problem, and it's transparent to shareholders.
留任确实是合理关切。与其采用金色降落伞——这可能传递错误信号——我建议一笔50万英镑的限制性股票单位留任授予，分四年等额归属。这与持续任职挂钩而非业绩，避免了不正当激励问题，对股东也是透明的。
A:That's a well-calibrated approach. So to summarise for the board: base salary of £690,000, annual bonus capped at 80% of base with 40% deferred and subject to clawback, performance shares vesting over three years tied to TSR and ROIC, and a one-time retention RSU grant of £500,000 vesting over four years. Total target compensation around £2.75 million at the 65th percentile.
这是一个精心校准的方案。总结给董事会：基本工资69万英镑，年度奖金上限为基本工资的80%且40%递延并受追回条款约束，绩效股三年归属与TSR和ROIC挂钩，一次性留任RSU授予50万英镑四年归属。目标薪酬总额约275万英镑，处于第65百分位。
B:Precisely. And I'd recommend the committee document the rationale for each component in the CD&A section of the proxy statement. When shareholders understand the "why," they're far less likely to vote against the compensation plan.
精准无误。我还建议薪酬委员会在股东委托书的CD&A部分记录每项构成的理论依据。当股东理解了"为什么"，他们投票反对薪酬方案的可能性就大大降低了。
A:Excellent. I'll present this framework to the full board next Tuesday. One administrative note — can your team prepare a competitive compensation heat map covering the top five executive roles? The board wants to ensure we're competitive at the senior VP level as well.
非常好。我将在下周二向全体董事会提交这一框架。一个行政事项——你的团队能否准备一份覆盖五大高管职位的竞争性薪酬热力图？董事会想确保我们在高级副总裁层面也具有竞争力。
B:We can have that heat map ready by Friday. I'll also include a sensitivity analysis — showing how compensation changes under different performance scenarios. It helps the board stress-test the plan before approval.
热力图周五前可以交出来。我还会附上一份敏感性分析——展示不同业绩情境下薪酬的变化。这有助于董事会在批准前对方案进行压力测试。
A:Perfect. This has been extremely productive, Mark. An independent perspective makes a world of difference in these discussions. Let's reconvene after the board reviews the proposal — I suspect they'll have follow-up questions on the retention structure.
完美。这次讨论非常高效，Mark。独立视角在这些讨论中至关重要。董事会审阅提案后我们再碰头——我估计他们会对留任结构有后续问题。

Key Vocabulary

total shareholder return (TSR) — 股东总回报 | 衡量公司为股东创造价值的综合指标，包含股价增长和股息
benchmarking — 对标分析 | 将公司薪酬水平与同行业/同规模企业进行系统比较的过程
long-term incentive (LTI) — 长期激励 | 以多年为周期、旨在绑定高管与公司长期利益的报酬形式
vesting period — 归属期/解锁期 | 股票或期权授予后需要等待才能实际拥有的时间阶段
clawback — 追回条款 | 在特定条件下（如业绩造假、重大风险事件）公司有权收回已发放薪酬的合同条款
golden parachute — 金色降落伞 | 高管在控制权变更或被解聘时可获高额补偿的合同条款
restricted stock units (RSUs) — 限制性股票单位 | 分阶段授予的股票，以持续任职为归属条件
pay-for-performance — 绩效薪酬 | 将薪酬与业绩指标直接挂钩的薪酬哲学
proxy statement — 股东委托书 | 上市公司在年度股东大会前向股东披露高管薪酬等关键信息的法律文件

Learning Tip

在BEC考试中，高管薪酬（Executive Compensation）是出现在商务阅读Part 5和听力Part 3中的公司治理话题。建议重点掌握薪酬结构中的"时间维度"表达——vesting（归属）、deferred（递延）、clawback（追回）这三个概念经常在完形填空中作为选项出现。阅读中遇到薪酬相关文章时，注意区分短期激励（STI）、长期激励（LTI）和基本薪资（base salary）的不同性质和作用。此外，"percentile"和"benchmarking"是在商业分析和报告中常用的量化表达，口语Part 3涉及公司政策讨论时灵活使用这些术语能显著提升专业性得分。
