BEC Daily

企业伦理 Corporate Ethics

A (Karen Mitchell) — Compliance Officer, BrightPath Financial
B (Jason Lee) — Sales VP, BrightPath Financial

Dialogue

A:Jason, thanks for making time. I need to discuss something that came across my desk from the quarterly audit. One of your regional managers, Daniel Park, has been entering client entertainment expenses that are well above our policy threshold — dinners exceeding £300 per head. Have you been aware of this?
Jason，感谢你抽出时间。我需要讨论一下季度审计中提交到我这里的一项事情。你的一位区域经理Daniel Park一直在录入远超我们政策上限的客户招待费用——每人超过300英镑的晚宴。你之前知道这个情况吗？
B:Karen, I appreciate the heads-up. I was aware Daniel has been aggressive with client hospitality, but I wasn't tracking the specifics. In his defence, the Southeast Asia market operates on a relationship-first model — what looks excessive in London can be standard practice in Kuala Lumpur. That said, if he's breaching policy, we need to address it.
Karen，感谢你提前告知。我知道Daniel在客户款待方面比较激进，但我没有追踪具体情况。为他辩护一下，东南亚市场遵循的是关系优先的模式——在伦敦看起来过分的款待可能在吉隆坡是标准做法。话虽如此，如果他违反了政策，我们必须处理。
A:I understand the cultural nuance, and I'm not suggesting we eliminate relationship-building. The issue is twofold: first, these expenses weren't pre-approved as our compliance handbook requires; second, three of the eight flagged dinners were with a client whose procurement manager is Daniel's brother-in-law. That crosses into conflict of interest territory, whether intentional or not.
我理解文化差异，也并非建议取消关系建设。问题有两方面：第一，这些费用没有按我们合规手册的要求进行事前审批；第二，被标记的八场晚宴中有三场是与一位客户进行的，而该客户的采购经理是Daniel的姐夫。这已经越界进入了利益冲突的领域，无论是否有意。
B:That's... not something I was aware of. The family connection certainly changes the optics. I should have been more diligent in reviewing his submissions. What's the recommended course of action from your side?
这……我之前确实不知道。亲属关系确实改变了事情的性质。我本应在审查他提交的材料时更加审慎。你这边建议的下一步行动方案是什么？
A:The company's code of conduct is clear on this. We need to launch a formal investigation — nothing punitive yet, but we have a fiduciary duty to our shareholders to ensure every client relationship is at arm's length. I'd recommend we suspend the expense privileges for Daniel's team pending review and conduct a refresher workshop on our anti-bribery policy for the entire sales division.
公司的行为准则对此有明确规定。我们需要启动正式调查——目前不是惩罚性质的，但我们对股东负有受托责任，要确保每一个客户关系都是公平交易。我建议在审查期间暂停Daniel团队的招待费用权限，并为整个销售部门举办关于反贿赂政策的复训工作坊。
B:I'll support that fully. The last thing we need is a regulatory breach showing up in an external audit. Let me be upfront — I should have caught this earlier, and I take accountability for that. What's our communication plan? We don't want this to spiral into office gossip before we've established the facts.
我全力支持。我们最不需要的就是外部审计中发现违规行为。让我坦诚以对——我本应更早发现这个问题，我为此承担责任。我们的沟通计划是什么？我们不希望这在我们查明事实之前演变成办公室流言。
A:Agreed. We'll keep it contained — you, me, HR, and legal. No broader announcement until the investigation concludes. In parallel, I want to propose we implement a mandatory quarterly ethics training module for all client-facing staff. It's not just about compliance — it's about embedding a speak-up culture where people feel safe flagging concerns before they escalate.
同意。我们会控制范围——你、我、人力资源和法务。调查结束前不做更广泛的公告。与此同时，我想提议我们对所有面向客户的员工实施每季度一次的强制性伦理培训模块。这不仅关乎合规——更关乎建立一种畅所欲言的文化，让人们在问题升级之前敢于提出。
B:I'm fully on board. In fact, I'd like to go a step further — let's establish an ethics committee with representatives from sales, compliance, and an external advisor. If we're going to rebuild trust, we need it to be visible and structural, not just procedural. Can I count on you to co-chair it with me?
我完全赞成。事实上，我想更进一步——我们建立一个伦理委员会，由销售、合规代表和一位外部顾问组成。如果我们要重建信任，就需要它是可见的、结构性的，而不仅仅是程序性的。你能和我共同主持吗？
A:I'd be honoured, Jason. That kind of top-down commitment is exactly what turns a compliance exercise into genuine cultural change. Let's draft the charter this week. And for what it's worth — the fact that you're leaning into this rather than deflecting makes all the difference.
我很荣幸，Jason。这种自上而下的承诺正是将合规流程转化为真正的文化变革的关键。我们本周起草章程。而且说实话——你选择正面面对而不是回避，这一点至关重要。
B:Appreciate that, Karen. At the end of the day, our reputation is our most valuable asset. Short-term revenue gains aren't worth compromising on integrity. Let's get this right and make BrightPath stronger for it.
感谢你，Karen。归根结底，声誉是我们最宝贵的资产。短期收入增长不值得以牺牲诚信为代价。让我们把这件事做好，让BrightPath因此变得更强大。

Key Vocabulary

compliance — 合规 | 企业确保自身行为符合法律法规和内部政策的过程，BEC核心话题
code of conduct — 行为准则 | 企业制定的道德和法律行为规范文件
conflict of interest — 利益冲突 | 个人利益与公司利益发生冲突的情形，伦理核心概念
fiduciary duty — 受托责任/信义义务 | 公司管理层对股东负有的法律和道德义务
at arm's length — 保持距离/公平交易 | 指交易双方独立、无特殊关系地进行商业往来
anti-bribery policy — 反贿赂政策 | 防止以不当利益换取商业优势的合规制度
regulatory breach — 违规/违反监管规定 | 企业行为违反法律法规或行业监管要求
speak-up culture — 畅所欲言的文化 | 鼓励员工在发现问题时主动报告的企业文化
due diligence — 尽职调查 | 在商业决策前对相关方进行彻底核查的过程
ethics committee — 伦理委员会 | 负责监督和指导企业道德事务的专门机构
integrity — 诚信/正直 | 企业在商业行为中坚持道德原则的品质

Sentence Patterns

I need to discuss something that came across my desk. → 我需要讨论一个提交到我这里的事项 | 合规官开启敏感话题的正式而中立的方式
The issue is twofold: first... second... → 问题有两个方面：第一……第二…… | 结构化陈述复杂问题的标准商务表达
That crosses into... territory, whether intentional or not. → 这已经越界进入了……领域，无论是否有意 | 明确指出行为触及红线，同时保留善意假设
I should have been more diligent in... → 我本应在……方面更加审慎 | 管理者承担责任时的自我批评表达
The last thing we need is... → 我们最不需要的就是…… | 强调某事的严重性和紧迫性
At the end of the day, our reputation is our most valuable asset. → 归根结底，声誉是我们最宝贵的资产 | 企业伦理讨论中的总结性和价值观表达

Learning Tip

企业伦理（Corporate Ethics）是BEC阅读Part 4和写作Part 2的重要主题，常见于报告和提案写作。建议重点掌握三层词汇体系：核心法律术语（compliance, fiduciary duty, regulatory breach）、道德判断用语（conflict of interest, at arm's length, integrity）以及行动表达（launch an investigation, take accountability, embed a culture）。在口语考试中，伦理话题常考察"如何回应不当行为"——关键展示从查明事实→承担责任→系统改进的完整思维链。
